# Level 1 worked cases
*Eight invented predictions scored against two invented plates, to show how Recognized, Invented, Net and Quantity (docs/METRICS.md, Level 1) behave. The fixtures live in `src/lib/scoring/cases.ts`; the table below is regenerated by `src/lib/scoring/cases.test.ts` — do not edit it by hand, run `pnpm test`.*

**Plate A (dinner):** salmon 180 g (core, weighed), sweet potato 160 g (core, weighed), roasted vegetables 210 g (secondary, weighed as one item with named components), olive oil 5 g (garnish, estimated after the hidden-fat check), curry powder and parsley (spice / garnish), salt and pepper (ignored).

**Plate B (breakfast):** oatmeal 220 g (core, converted from dry weight), honey 15 g (secondary), blueberries 60 g (secondary).

What the cases are meant to show:

1. A plain image-only answer: right foods, grams too high, one small invented item.
2. The same plate with context: the gain is mostly in Quantity.
3. The protein is missed but every garnish is found: item-level F1 looks fine, Net and Quantity do not.
4. A substitute name (trout for salmon) costs Recognized, not Quantity.
5. A confident wrong item (chicken for salmon) is worse than a miss: it counts as missed **and** invented.
6. A padded log (four small phantom items) costs little.
7. A small but consequential miss (honey) is hidden by mass weighting; Recognized flags it, Levels 2–3 carry the consequence.
8. Context only, on a routine meal, lands close to case 2.
